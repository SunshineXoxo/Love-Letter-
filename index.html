A letter from my heart

Dear Cat, my Thandeka.

Three months of us. Ten months of knowing you. A million little reasons to be grateful.

✿23 October · our little milestone✿

My love,

Sometimes I wish I could take everything I feel for you, place it gently in your hands, and let you see it the way I see it. Maybe then you would understand why even the smallest moments with you have found a permanent home in my heart.

We've known each other for ten months, and now we've had three beautiful months of being us. I know three months might sound small to the world, but what matters to me is the life we've already tucked into that time: our ice-cream dates, our car dates, the times we went to town and ate sadza, and the day you cooked sadza and pork for me. You made a meal, but what I remember most is how cared for I felt.

I remember us getting our nails done and then going for burgers at RocoMamas. I remember all those ordinary little adventures that somehow never feel ordinary when I'm with you. And I especially remember that restaurant day when you introduced me to subs. They were amazing, yes — but the memory is special because I got to try something new with you. You have a way of turning a simple day into a story I want to keep.

What I love most about you is your heart. You keep giving. You are patient with me when I'm in a bad mood, kind even when I'm not making it easy, and loving in ways you don't owe me but choose to give anyway. You take care of me, you listen to my stories — even the ones that probably need a whole season and a recap — and you make me feel like what I have to say matters.

I love your beautiful face, your smile, and the way you answer my calls with “hey babe.” It is such a small thing, but it can make my whole day feel softer. I hope you know that you deserve the same patience, gentleness, care, and love that you keep giving to me. I want to keep learning how to love you well, not just say that I do.

And of course, there is us being unserious. 😂 When money or transport money disappears, somehow we still manage to look at the situation and say, “takabva tese kumadhaka.” At this point, it deserves to be in our relationship constitution. The playful taps, the teasing, and the way we can laugh together are part of my favourite version of life.

Now, I know you sometimes have competition for my attention from one Kylian Mbappé. Let the record show that he may have a place in football history, but you have a place in my heart that no footballer, no Wi-Fi outage, and no network failure can touch. You are my favourite person, Cat. 😂💗

Since you're a network engineer, let me put it this way: my heart has found its favourite endpoint, the connection is strong, and I am not looking for another network. No packet loss could make me forget the little things that make you you. And if love were an information system, you would be the feature I would never deprecate.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for the laughter, the food, the dates, the listening, the softness, and the care. Thank you for letting me know you and for building this little world with me. I don't expect us to be perfect every day. I just hope we keep choosing honesty, patience, laughter, and each other — especially on the days that ask a little more from us.

Happy three months, my love. Ten months since you became part of my story, and I am so glad you did. I can't promise every day will be easy, but I can promise that what I feel for you is real, and that I want to keep showing you in the ways that matter.

Here's to more ice cream, more car dates, more food adventures, more inside jokes, more memories with our locs looking good, and many more “hey babe” moments. Here's to growing together, one little day at a time.

I love you, Thandeka. More than this little website can ever fit. ♡

Yours, always trying to be better at loving you,
